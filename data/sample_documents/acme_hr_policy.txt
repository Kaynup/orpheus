ACME CORPORATION EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & HR POLICY
=================================================

1. INTRODUCTION
Welcome to Acme Corporation. This Employee Handbook provides a comprehensive overview of our policies, benefits, and workplace expectations. It is intended to serve as a definitive guide for all full-time and part-time employees. Please read it thoroughly, as it supersedes all prior verbal or written policies.

2. REMOTE WORK AND HYBRID ARRANGEMENTS
Acme Corporation is committed to fostering a flexible work environment that balances individual productivity with team collaboration. We operate on a hybrid model designed to maximize both focused work and synchronous communication.

2.1 Location Independence
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Remote work is a privilege and depends on sustained performance and role requirements. To ensure seamless communication across distributed teams, we enforce standard core collaboration hours. All employees must be online and available for synchronous meetings from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time (ET). This overlap guarantees that essential operational decisions can be made without asynchronous delays.

2.2 In-Office Requirements
For teams operating on the hybrid schedule, in-office presence is mandatory to facilitate cross-functional planning and team building. The mandatory in-office days are Tuesdays and Thursdays. During these days, employees are expected to participate in all-hands meetings, sprint planning, and collaborative whiteboarding sessions at their designated local hub.

2.3 Home Office Equipment
Acme recognizes that a productive remote work environment requires adequate equipment. Upon passing the probationary period, all full-time employees are eligible for a one-time home office equipment stipend of $750. This stipend may be applied toward ergonomic chairs, external monitors, standing desks, or upgraded networking hardware. Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal within 90 days of the purchase.

3. BENEFITS AND LEAVE
Acme Corporation offers a competitive suite of benefits to support our employees' health, well-being, and work-life balance.

3.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
We believe in the importance of rest. Employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, along with 10 days of sick leave. Unused sick leave does not roll over. 

3.2 Parental Leave
Welcoming a new child is a major life event, and we are dedicated to supporting growing families. Acme provides up to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for birth, adoption, or foster placement. This leave may be taken consecutively or split into two separate periods within the first year of the child's arrival. Employees must notify HR at least 30 days prior to the anticipated start date to ensure adequate coverage. Parental leave runs concurrently with any FMLA entitlements.

4. CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional integrity, respect, and compliance with local laws. Detailed policies regarding anti-harassment, conflict of interest, and data privacy can be found on the internal company intranet.
