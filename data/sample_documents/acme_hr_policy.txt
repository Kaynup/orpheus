# ACME CORPORATION - EMPLOYEE POLICIES & GUIDELINES HANDBOOK (2025)

## 1. Work Schedule and Core Hours
Acme Corporation operates on a flexible 40-hour work week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time (ET), Monday through Friday. All full-time employees are expected to be available for team meetings and customer inquiries during these core hours.

## 2. Remote Work and Hybrid Policy
Employees eligible for remote work may work remotely up to three (3) days per week with manager approval. 
To maintain team cohesion, Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated as In-Office Collaboration Days for hybrid team members located within 30 miles of an Acme regional office. Fully remote employees must maintain high-speed internet connectivity (minimum 50 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload) and receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of $750.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leaves
All full-time employees receive twenty (20) days of accrued PTO annually during their first three years of service, increasing to twenty-five (25) days starting in year four. 
In addition to standard PTO, Acme offers:
- 12 paid corporate holidays per calendar year.
- 16 weeks of fully paid Parental Leave for primary and secondary caregivers following birth, adoption, or foster placement.
- 5 days of paid bereavement leave.

## 4. Health and Wellness Benefits
Acme provides 100% employer-covered premium health, dental, and vision insurance for individual employees, and covers 80% of premiums for dependent coverage. 
Employees also receive an annual $600 Wellness Reimbursement for gym memberships, fitness equipment, or mental health apps.

## 5. Travel and Expense Reimbursement
All business travel expenses must be pre-approved by a department director. 
Daily meal allowance (per diem) is capped at $85 per day in standard metropolitan areas and $110 per day in high-cost cities (e.g., New York, San Francisco, London). 
Hotel accommodations must not exceed $250 per night excluding taxes without prior executive exception.
Expense reports must be submitted within thirty (30) days of travel completion with itemized receipts.
